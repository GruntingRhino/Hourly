# Notice, consent and version evidence requirements

> Drafting note: legal applicability and operator obligations require counsel and school approval; this document records proposed controls, not certification.[1]

Every published Privacy Notice, Terms, school notice, parent form, student attestation, DPA, and integration authorization must have immutable version id, effective timestamp/time zone, content hash, publication URL, language, approver, school/pilot scope, and supersession link. At acceptance record user/account, school, document version/hash, timestamp, flow, and consent/attestation text identifier.

Keep evidence in access-controlled storage. Support export for the school, correction of metadata without rewriting history, and legal hold. Do not infer consent from continued use when affirmative acknowledgment is required. Do not backfill evidence from a UI screenshot.

Before release: diff public text against implementation; verify provider/register; verify 13+ gate behavior; test withdrawal/revocation and deletion; obtain school approval and counsel review; archive signed PDFs/forms; record deployment SHA and migration; publish only after owner checklist is green.
