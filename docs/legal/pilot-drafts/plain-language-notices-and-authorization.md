# Plain-language pilot notices and authorization forms

> Drafting note: legal applicability and operator obligations require counsel and school approval; this document records proposed controls, not certification.[1]

## School administrator notice
GoodHours helps the school organize and verify community-service hours. The school decides who may use it, what staff can see, which organizations participate, how long records remain, and how records are corrected or exported. The system may handle names, emails, school/cohort information, attendance and hours, messages, signatures/files, reports, imports/exports, and optional LMS data. The school should use the attached agreement/addendum to direct the operator. Staff should not upload unnecessary sensitive information. Ask the school—not the student—to approve parent sharing or changes to official records.

Before launch the school must confirm: legal authority and notices; 13+ gate; staff roles; approved organizations; parent/guardian workflow; retention/deletion; emergency/safeguarding contacts; integration scopes; incident contact; accessibility; and how a student requests access/correction.

## Student notice
You must be at least 13 for this pilot. Do not create an account for someone else or enter a false age. GoodHours records opportunities, attendance, hours and approvals for your school. School-authorized staff can review records; approved organizations may see only information needed to verify their activity. Messages are for school/community-service coordination, not private or emergency communication. Do not share passwords, your home address, private phone number, photos, or secret contact details. Report unsafe, sexual, threatening, discriminatory, or suspicious messages using the school’s reporting channel.

Your school controls official education-record decisions. You may ask the school to review/correct/export a record. You may ask GoodHours about account data, but deletion may be limited when the school must retain a record.

## Parent/guardian notice (use only when the school chooses)
The school is inviting your child to a supervised service-hour system. This form does not itself decide whether consent is legally required, nor does it replace school authority or a school-record request process. The school should identify the applicable basis and whether it wants authorization. GoodHours will not use the information for advertising or unrelated profiling under the pilot terms. The school will explain what is collected, who sees it, retention, integrations, correction, export, deletion limits, and how to withdraw/ask questions.
