# GoodHours Privacy Notice (pilot draft)
**Proposed effective date:** NEEDS OWNER INPUT | **Operator:** NEEDS OWNER INPUT

## Scope and role clarity
This notice explains the proposed handling of information in the GoodHours pilot for students, school staff, service organizations, and other authorized users. It does not claim that GoodHours is FERPA- or COPPA-compliant. For school-controlled student records, the school/district must determine its legal authority and instruct GoodHours through a signed agreement. FERPA access depends on the applicable exception and the school’s control; school staff access is not automatically unrestricted.[1][12][16]

## Information the product may process
Based on inspected product paths and current UI claims, the pilot may process: account name/email/password hash or Google identifier; school, cohort, grade or other profile fields actually enabled; invitations and roster/CSV imports; service opportunities, signups, attendance check-in/out, hours, approvals/rejections, verifier identity and timestamps; self-submissions and notes; messages/notifications; uploaded signatures/files; audit/access logs; LMS identifiers, course/roster data and encrypted integration credentials where an integration is enabled; email delivery metadata; billing/procurement data if billing is enabled.

**Minimization rule:** do not collect date of birth or identity documents merely to enforce 13+. Store only an age-eligibility attestation and its evidence metadata unless a documented legal/safety need is approved.


> Citation sources are maintained in the package-local ledger at `evidence/ledger.json`; this draft uses only official sources retrieved on 2026-09-05.


## Age and school-authority statement
GoodHours proposes a 13+ account policy. A user statement is an eligibility attestation, not verified age. GoodHours does not require parent consent for every 13–17-year-old account under this draft; the school may impose stricter participation rules. No under-13 exception is offered. This policy does not decide whether COPPA applies; FTC materials emphasize parental control for young children and purpose-limited retention, while the 2025 amendment and compliance timing require current counsel review.[2][3][4]

## Requests, incidents, and deletion
Use the request procedure supplied by the school or the operator contact shown at publication. The school controls education-record access, amendment, and school-authorized disclosures; GoodHours will assist under documented instruction. For a Massachusetts public-school pilot, the package also treats 603 CMR 23.00 as a source requiring reconciliation of confidentiality, access, amendment, access-log, notification, and destruction workflows with the school’s approved policy.[12][16][17] The package also incorporates the regulation’s access-log, amendment, and notification sections into the school-controlled workflow.[18][19] Report suspected safety issues through the school’s safeguarding channel and urgent danger through emergency services. If a security incident is confirmed, the operator will provide available facts and updates to the school contact without promising a universal deadline; notice timing depends on law, facts, and school direction.
