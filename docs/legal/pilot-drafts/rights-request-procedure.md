# Data access, correction, export, deletion and disclosure-request procedure

## Intake
Accept requests through the verified privacy/support channel; do not publish the placeholder emails until ownership is confirmed. Record requester role, school, account identifier, request type, date, preferred safe contact, and authentication evidence. Never request passwords or full identity documents by email.

## Routing
- Education-record access/amendment, parent access, official export, and school retention: route to school administrator; operator assists. FERPA rights and disclosure accounting depend on context and exception. For a Massachusetts public-school pilot, the school workflow must also reconcile 603 CMR 23.07 access logs and request timing, 603 CMR 23.08 amendment procedure, and the applicable destruction schedule.[1][12][17][18]
- Account/profile access, transactional data, deletion, or provider question: operator privacy owner, with school notice where student data is involved.
- Safety/abuse: safeguarding lead and school immediately; emergency risk goes to local emergency services/school protocol, not platform chat.

## Decision workflow
1. Authenticate proportionately. 2. Identify controller/school and affected records. 3. Search authoritative stores, imports, uploads, logs, integrations, email queues, and backups according to the approved schedule. 4. Apply school instruction, legal hold, third-party privacy, security, and immutable-record constraints. 5. Produce readable access copy or CSV export; explain fields omitted. 6. Correct by logged amendment/reversal, never silently rewrite verified history. 7. Delete/minimize only when authorized; record exceptions. 8. Close with decision, date, scope, escalation route, and evidence.

## Request forms
**Access/export:** requester; role/school; records sought; date range; safe delivery channel; authorization.
**Correction:** record identifier; alleged error; proposed correction; evidence; school decision.
**Deletion:** records/account; reason; school awareness; requested date; acknowledgment that official records/legal holds may remain.
**Disclosure accounting:** school requester; date range; student/record; requested recipients/purpose.

Target response times: NEEDS OWNER INPUT; never promise a statutory deadline without jurisdiction analysis.

## Operational decision tree (proposed)
1. Intake: record request ID, date/time, requester channel, account identifiers, school, requested action, urgency, and attachments. Do not request a full DOB or identity document unless counsel approves it as necessary.
2. Triage: route School Data requests to the School contact; route a direct consumer-account request to the operator contact; route safety, subpoena, or law-enforcement requests to the designated legal contact. Acknowledge receipt without promising entitlement or deadline.
3. Authority: School verifies whether the requester is a parent, eligible student, school official, authorized representative, or other person under its policy. Operator records the School decision and does not substitute a self-attestation for school authority.
4. Search/preserve: identify account, roster, hours, messages, uploads, integrations, exports, audit events, and applicable backups. Freeze deletion only for the requested scope and a documented legal hold.
5. Decision: School chooses access/export, correction (with disputed value preserved where required), deletion/restriction, or denial with reason. For a FERPA amendment request, the School—not Operator—controls the hearing/amendment process. FERPA sources describe school control and legitimate educational-interest limits; they do not create a universal operator-side parent-consent rule.[1]
6. Execute and evidence: perform the approved action, log actor/time/scope/result, notify requester through the approved channel, and record residual copies or exceptions. Escalate ambiguity to counsel rather than silently deleting or disclosing.
