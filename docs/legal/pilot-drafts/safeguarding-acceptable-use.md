# Acceptable-use, community messaging and safeguarding policy

> Drafting note: legal applicability and operator obligations require counsel and school approval; this document records proposed controls, not certification.[1]

## Allowed use
Use GoodHours for school-approved service opportunities, attendance, hour verification, school-organized messaging, reports, and authorized integrations.

## Prohibited conduct
No harassment, bullying, sexual content or solicitation, grooming, threats, hate, doxxing, stalking, impersonation, fraud, forged signatures, false hours, malware, scraping, credential sharing, attempts to bypass age/school controls, or moving a minor interaction off approved channels to evade supervision. Do not request or post home addresses, personal phone numbers, passwords, financial data, medical details, or intimate images.

## Supervision boundaries
GoodHours is not an emergency service. Organizations must provide lawful adult supervision and safe placements; GoodHours does not background-check or guarantee an organization unless a separate verified process says so. Staff should use school-approved channels and not privately contact minors.

## Report and triage
Report in-product if available or to the school safeguarding contact and verified GoodHours safety contact. Preserve message links/screenshots without redistributing sensitive content. P0/P1: restrict account/content, preserve evidence, notify school lead and safety owner, assess emergency escalation. Do not promise confidentiality where safety or law requires disclosure.

## Fair process
Temporary restrictions may be imposed to protect people or records. Provide a school-directed review path, avoid retaliation, minimize disclosure, and document decisions.
