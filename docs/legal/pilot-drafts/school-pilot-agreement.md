# GoodHours School Pilot Agreement (draft for LSRHS review)
**School:** LSRHS — legal entity/district and signatory NEEDS OWNER INPUT. **Operator:** NEEDS OWNER INPUT.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Operator will provide a controlled pilot for community-service opportunity coordination, student enrollment, attendance/check-in/out, hour verification, messaging, reminders, reports/CSV export, imports, uploads/signatures, and only separately approved Classroom/Canvas, email, and billing features. The parties must mark each feature enabled; “full functionality” does not authorize silent activation of an integration or vendor.

## 2. School control
School determines eligible users, roles, cohorts, approved organizations, educational purpose, records policy, retention, parent/guardian communications, corrections, exports, holds, and termination. Operator processes student data only on documented school instructions and may not sell, advertise to, profile, or use it for unrelated purposes. The school remains able to access, correct, export, restrict, and direct return/deletion. These terms are intended to operationalize school control; counsel must confirm whether they satisfy the applicable FERPA exception.[1]

## 3. Responsibilities
School supplies lawful enrollment/authorization, staff training, role approvals, safeguarding escalation contacts, and instructions. Operator provides the agreed service, role-based access, security measures, request assistance, incident notice, subprocessor transparency, and evidence of deletion/return. Neither party guarantees that the other’s legal obligations are satisfied merely by signing.

## 4. Student/parent notices
School approves plain-language notices and any authorization workflow before enrollment. Parent/guardian involvement is determined by the school’s policy and applicable law; this agreement does not assert universal consent requirements.

## 5. Security and incidents
Operator maintains the internal program in `security-and-incident-response.md`, reports suspected incidents without undue delay under the agreed matrix, preserves evidence, coordinates investigation, and does not make public statements naming the school without approval except as legally required. No universal 72-hour promise is made.

## 6. Subprocessors and integrations
Only listed/approved subprocessors and school-authorized LMS scopes may be used. New subprocessors require notice and the school’s defined objection/approval process. Provider location, subprocessors, deletion, support access, and contract terms must be completed in the register.

## 7. Retention, return, deletion, termination
At school direction and termination, Operator returns an export in an agreed format and deletes copies on the proposed schedule, subject to legal holds, security logs, backups, and school-controlled record exceptions. “Delete” means logical/cryptographic/physical treatment as documented; no deletion SLA is promised until implemented and tested.

## 8. Operative school-control terms (to become binding only on signature)
(a) School determines the educational purpose, permitted fields, user population, access roles, retention schedule, and disclosure instructions. Operator will process School Data only on documented School instructions in this Agreement, an approved implementation ticket, or a written emergency instruction confirmed afterward. School retains responsibility for deciding which records are education records and for responding to parent/eligible-student access or amendment requests.

(b) Operator will not sell School Data, use it for advertising, combine it with unrelated customer data for profiling, or redisclose it except to an approved subprocessor or integration acting on School instructions. Operator will require each person with access to confidentiality obligations and role-based need-to-know access. The school-official theory requires the school to retain direct control over use and maintenance and to limit officials to legitimate educational interests; counsel must confirm the facts and selected FERPA exception before signing.[1]

(c) Operator will maintain a current subprocessor/data-flow register, obtain School approval for material additions, and give the School a practical objection and migration/termination option. No subprocessor is approved merely because it appears in this draft.

(d) Operator will notify the School contact without undue delay after confirming unauthorized access, acquisition, disclosure, alteration, or loss of School Data, and will provide known facts, affected data categories, containment steps, and updates. The parties will decide whether and when notices to families, regulators, or individuals are legally required; this Agreement does not promise a universal deadline or permit delaying notice solely until every fact is confirmed.

(e) On termination, Operator will return an export in the agreed format, identify residual copies, and delete production copies within **[number of days—owner/counsel]**, except records subject to a documented legal hold, immutable backup rotation, or a narrowly scoped security record. Backups will be isolated from ordinary use and deleted or overwritten on the documented rotation; restoration may reintroduce records only under the School's written instruction.

(f) Audit evidence will be limited to reasonable documentation, questionnaires, and a mutually agreed assessment; it will not provide unrestricted access to other customers or secrets.
