# Student Data Privacy Addendum (DPA draft)

## A. Definitions and roles
“School Data” includes student education records, directory/profile data, roster, attendance, hours, messages, uploads, identifiers, logs, and integration data supplied by or for the school. School is controller/educational authority as determined by counsel; Operator is processor/service provider/school official only to the extent the executed agreement and law support that role.

## B. Processing instructions
Purpose: operate the approved GoodHours pilot and nothing else. Operator may process only documented fields, roles, duration, and operations in the agreement/register. No sale, targeted advertising, unrelated profiling, model training on identifiable School Data, or redisclosure. Operator must ensure personnel confidentiality and least privilege.

## C. Direct control and rights
School may access, review, correct, export, restrict, and direct deletion/return. Operator assists with FERPA access/amendment requests, parent/student inquiries, investigations, and legally required disclosure accounting. A disclosure log must capture recipient, date, fields/purpose, authority, and operator/school decision where applicable.[1]

## D. Security
Operator applies the documented internal program: unique accounts, RBAC/school scoping, secure authentication, encryption where verified, secrets management, upload validation, logging, rate limiting, vulnerability management, backups/restore decisions, and incident response. Any control not verified is a proposed commitment, not a representation.

## E. Subprocessors and transfers
Subprocessors require documented purpose, fields, location, contract terms, confidentiality, security, incident cooperation, return/deletion, and school notice/objection. The register is authoritative only after owner verification.

## F. Incidents
Operator notifies School’s designated contact promptly after confirmation of a security incident involving School Data, provides known facts, scope, containment, affected categories, and remediation, updates as facts change, and preserves evidence. Legal notification timing is decided by the matrix, not a blanket 72-hour term.

## G. Return/deletion and audit
On instruction/termination, return data in the agreed export, delete active copies, address backups under the approved schedule, and certify completion. School may request reasonable evidence/audit subject to security and confidentiality. Legal holds and immutable audit records are disclosed exceptions, not hidden exclusions.

## H. Binding instruction schedule (complete before execution)
The School is the documented decision-maker for: (1) purpose—community-service coordination, attendance/check-in, hour verification, authorized messaging and reporting; (2) fields—only those listed in the approved data inventory; (3) roles—named school administrators, teachers, cohort-limited staff, students, service organizations, and support personnel; (4) integrations—only the approved Google Classroom/Canvas, email, storage, billing, and other providers listed in the register; and (5) retention, legal holds, export format, and deletion dates. Blank items are not approved instructions.

Operator must: follow those instructions; notify the School if an instruction appears unlawful or technically impossible; keep access least-privilege and confidential; assist with access, correction, deletion, disclosure-accounting, and incident investigations; and make relevant compliance information available for a reasonable school audit. The School decides whether a request belongs to a parent, eligible student, school official, or another authorized requester; Operator must not independently promise a requester access to education records. FERPA materials describe direct school control and legitimate educational-interest limits, but they do not decide this product's legal status.[1]

## I. Redisclosure and rights workflow
Operator will not disclose School Data to a new recipient or use it for a new purpose without the School's written instruction and counsel-approved basis. Any permitted recipient must receive purpose-limited instructions and must not further disclose. The School maintains any required disclosure record and decides the response to parent/eligible-student requests; Operator will search, preserve, export, correct, restrict, or delete only as instructed and subject to a documented hold.

## J. Incident and return/deletion commitments
The incident process in `security-and-incident-response.md` is incorporated as a proposed operating procedure. It requires prompt internal escalation, containment, evidence preservation, a decision log, and School updates with known facts and uncertainty. It does not promise a fixed 72-hour rule. On expiry or termination, Operator returns and deletes according to the binding schedule, records exceptions, and does not treat routine backups or security logs as a license to retain the full dataset indefinitely.
