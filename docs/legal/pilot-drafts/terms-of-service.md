## 1. Contracting and eligibility
GoodHours is a school/service-hour coordination tool, not an employer, placement agency, verifier of real-world safety, or guarantee that a school accepts a record. The pilot is limited to users **13 or older**. A user must complete the displayed affirmative eligibility statement and accept the notice version before account activation; the statement is not a verified age determination.

A minor’s ability to contract, assent, and bind themselves varies by jurisdiction and circumstance. These Terms do not pretend that a minor’s click creates an enforceable contract for every purpose. The school agreement governs school use; a parent/guardian authorization may be required by the school or applicable law, but is not a blanket substitute for school approval or a universal prerequisite asserted here. For users under 18, school participation and any guardian authorization process do not waive non-waivable rights or authorize unrelated data use.

No under-13 user may enter the pilot. Known or reported under-13 cases are handled under the eligibility specification, without coaching evasion.

## 2. School and organization responsibilities
Schools control enrollment, staff permissions, records policy, and requests relating to school records. Organizations must be truthful, supervise minors lawfully, publish accurate opportunities, approve only performed hours, and report safety concerns. Schools must identify authorized staff and approve integrations.

## 3. Student duties and community conduct
Users must provide truthful hours, not forge signatures, impersonate, harass, exploit, dox, threaten, groom, solicit private contact, or move minor interactions off school-approved channels to evade supervision. Reports may be made through the safeguarding policy.

## 4. Records and changes
Verification and audit records may be immutable or school-controlled and may require correction rather than deletion. The school’s approved policy controls official records; GoodHours does not promise that every user deletion request erases a school record.

## 5. Privacy, integrations, suspension
The Privacy Notice and signed school/addendum terms govern data handling. Integrations require authorization and can be revoked. GoodHours may suspend access to protect users, records, or security, using the least disruptive process consistent with safety and school direction.

## 6. Disclaimers and liability
Use is provided as-is to the maximum permitted by law. Do not include arbitration, class waiver, indemnity, governing-law, or liability caps until reviewed for minors, schools, and the operator’s jurisdiction; the current UI’s Delaware/arbitration language is not carried forward as an approved term.
